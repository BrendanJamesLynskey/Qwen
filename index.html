Qwen — Presentation Series

The Qwen Models

Alibaba's open-weight model family, from the 7B research release of August 2023 to a 2.4-trillion-parameter flagship three years later. Twelve deep-dive decks covering every generation and the models within each — architecture, benchmarks, abilities, flags, honest strengths and weaknesses, and exactly how well each is served by vLLM. Written for engineers who have to choose, deploy and operate these models.

The Series

Five sections. The first three walk the family forward in time, so that each architectural idea arrives with the problem it was built to solve. The last two turn all of it into deployment decisions. Every deck stands alone — if you only need Qwen3-Coder or the vLLM reference, start there.

How to read this series

Read Part 01 first for the map. Parts 02–04 build the vocabulary — tokenizers, grouped-query attention, mixtures of experts, reasoning traces — that the later decks assume. Part 05 is the pivot: Qwen3 is the generation everything since is defined against. Part 07 is the most important architectural deck; understand the 3:1 hybrid stack and you understand every current Qwen. Parts 11 and 12 are reference material rather than narrative, and are worth returning to.

If you are here for one thing: Qwen3-Coder is Part 06, vLLM serving is Part 11, and which model should I use is Part 12.
