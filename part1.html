As of

This series is written as of 10 August 2026. The Qwen line moves faster than almost any other; every factual claim about a specific model is dated, and anything still in flux — the Qwen3.8 open-weights drop in particular — is flagged as such rather than stated as settled.

01

Why Qwen is the one open family you must know

If you build on open weights, you almost certainly already depend on Qwen — possibly without knowing it. The family has become the default base model for the open fine-tuning ecosystem in the way Llama was in 2023–24, and for a specific set of reasons that are worth naming explicitly.

The four structural advantages

  • Full size ladder, every generation. Qwen ships sub-1B through frontier-scale in one release, trained on one data pipeline with one tokenizer. You can prototype on a 4B, deploy a 30B, and escalate to a 400B without changing your prompt format, chat template, or tool-call schema.
  • Apache 2.0, consistently. Since Qwen2, the overwhelming majority of open Qwen weights carry a plain Apache 2.0 licence — no acceptable-use rider, no monthly-active-user threshold, no naming obligation. For commercial deployment that removes an entire class of legal review.
  • Same-week inference support. Qwen ships with vLLM and SGLang support landed or landing on day one, plus GGUF, MLX and AWQ/FP8 checkpoints. Compare that to families where community quantisation lags the release by weeks.
  • Genuine breadth. Text, code, vision, audio, omni, embeddings, rerankers, safety classifiers and image generation — all from one team, sharing a tokenizer and a chat template.
The scale of it

Hugging Face now hosts on the order of 200,000 derivative models built on Qwen bases — fine-tunes, merges, quantisations and distillations. Qwen3-VL-2B-Instruct alone has passed 18 million downloads. When a paper says "we fine-tuned a 7B open model", the odds are it was a Qwen.

02

The house that Tongyi built

Qwen is the model family of Alibaba Cloud's Tongyi Lab. The Chinese product name is Tongyi Qianwen (通义千问, roughly "truth from a thousand questions"); "Qwen" is the contraction used for the open-weight releases and the English-language brand. The distinction still matters in 2026, because the two names track two different distribution channels.

Tongyi Qianwen / Model Studio

The hosted, commercial side. Alibaba Cloud Model Studio (DashScope) serves the proprietary -Plus and -Max tiers over an OpenAI-compatible API, plus the consumer Qwen App and Qwen Chat. This is where the frontier lands first.

Qwen / Hugging Face & ModelScope

The open-weight side. Checkpoints, technical reports and reference code published to Hugging Face, ModelScope and GitHub, almost always Apache 2.0, almost always with a same-day vLLM/SGLang recipe.

The public timeline starts on 11 April 2023, when Alibaba announced Tongyi Qianwen as a hosted assistant. The moment that actually mattered to engineers came four months later: on 3 August 2023 the team open-sourced Qwen-7B and Qwen-7B-Chat. At that point China had no credible open frontier-adjacent model; within eighteen months Qwen had become the reference base for a very large fraction of the world's open fine-tunes.

Two organisational facts explain the release cadence. First, the team ships base and instruct checkpoints together, so the research community can build immediately. Second, they treat the open releases as a genuine product line rather than a marketing exercise — the open models get the technical reports, the benchmarks, and the serving support, not just a weights dump.

03

The timeline, by product line

Reading the Qwen release history as a single sequence is confusing, because five or six product lines run in parallel and each has its own cadence. Read it as swimlanes instead and the structure becomes obvious: a core LLM line that sets the architecture, specialist lines that fork off it, and — from late 2025 — a proprietary flagship tier that runs ahead of the open weights.

Two shapes stand out. The specialist lanes converge back into the core — QwQ's reasoning became Qwen3's thinking mode; the separate VL line was absorbed by natively multimodal Qwen3.5. And from September 2025 the Max tier runs ahead: the strongest Qwen is now announced as a hosted API first, with open weights following at a lag, if at all.

04

How to read a Qwen model name

Qwen model IDs are dense but entirely systematic. Once you can parse one you can infer the deployment footprint, the serving flags and the behaviour without opening the model card.

The two suffixes that change behaviour, not just size

  • -2507-style date stamps. A four-digit YYMM tag marks a re-post-trained refresh of an existing base — Qwen3-235B-A22B-Instruct-2507 is July 2025's retrain. These are not minor: the 2507 line dropped hybrid thinking, doubled usable context to 262K, and materially changed benchmark standing.
  • -Next. Reserved for architecture-preview models that break from the generation's standard design. Qwen3-Next-80B-A3B was where hybrid linear attention was proved out before it became the mainline design in Qwen3.5.
Rule of thumb

For an MoE, total parameters buy you VRAM cost; active parameters buy you latency. Qwen3.6-35B-A3B holds 35B of weights but does 3B of work per token — roughly a 4090-class memory bill with a 3B-class token rate. That decoupling is the single most important thing to internalise about the modern Qwen line.

The three-rung ladder: open weights, Plus, Max

Since Qwen3-Max (September 2025) the family has been explicitly tiered. Knowing which rung a model sits on tells you its licence, its context limit and whether you can ever self-host it.

The 2026 tier ladder in practice

  • Qwen3-Max (Sept 2025) — the first Qwen past a trillion parameters, and the first to be API-only.
  • Qwen3.6-Max-Preview (April 2026) — ~1T sparse MoE, 262K context, agentic-coding focused.
  • Qwen3.7-Max (May 2026) — ~1T total / ~24B active, 1M context. The agent-frontier flagship for a quarter.
  • Qwen3.8-Max (3 Aug 2026) — 2.4T total / 95B active, 1M context, native vision. Priced at $2 / $6 per million input / output tokens with $0.25 cached reads.
The open question of 2026

Alibaba stated at the Qwen3.8-Max launch that weights for both Qwen3.8-Max and a Qwen3.8-27B sibling would be published — which would be the first open release at Max scale. As of this writing the repositories had not appeared and no licence had been named. Treat it as announced, not shipped. Part 10 covers what would change if it lands.

06

Licensing — the long march to Apache 2.0

Qwen's licence history is worth knowing because it determines whether models from a given era are usable commercially without review, and because it explains why so much downstream tooling standardised on Qwen specifically.

2023 — Tongyi Qianwen License

A bespoke source-available licence with a monthly-active-user threshold above which a separate commercial agreement was required. The 1.8B shipped under a research-only variant. Usable, but it needed a lawyer.

2024 — the split

Qwen2 put the 57B-A14B, 7B, 1.5B and 0.5B under Apache 2.0 while the 72B flagship stayed proprietary. Qwen2.5 kept the same pattern — most sizes Apache, the 3B under a research licence, the 72B restricted.

2025–26 — Apache everywhere

From Qwen3 onwards every open-weight release, up to and including the 397B-A17B Qwen3.5 flagship, ships plain Apache 2.0. The restriction migrated from the licence to the tier: the strongest models simply are not released.

That final move is the strategically interesting one. Rather than gating a released model with licence terms, Alibaba now gates by publication: everything published is maximally permissive, and commercial advantage is preserved in the unpublished Max tier. For an engineer that is a much cleaner contract — if you can download it, you can ship it.

Practical check

Always read the LICENSE file in the specific repository rather than assuming from the generation. Legacy Qwen1/Qwen1.5/Qwen2-72B checkpoints are still on Hugging Face under the old terms, and derivative fine-tunes inherit whatever the base carried.

07

The architectural through-line

Strip away the model names and the family tells one clear story in three acts: a conventional dense transformer, a pivot to sparsity, and then a pivot to sub-quadratic attention. Each act was driven by a specific cost that had become the binding constraint.

The Act III payoff is not subtle. Alibaba reports Qwen3.5 decoding roughly 8.6× faster at 32K context and 19× faster at 256K than Qwen3-Max on identical hardware. When 90% of a coding agent's tokens are prefill over a large repository, that is the difference between a viable product and an unaffordable one.

08

The ecosystem around the weights

Weights alone do not make a family usable. Qwen's practical advantage is that the surrounding tooling is first-party, open, and released in lockstep with the models.

Qwen-Agent

The reference agent framework — function calling, MCP client support, a code interpreter, RAG and a browser extension. It is also the canonical implementation of Qwen's tool-call prompt format, which is useful even if you never import it.

Qwen Code

A terminal coding agent, originally adapted from Gemini CLI with a rewritten parser and tool layer, tuned specifically for the Qwen3-Coder function-call format. The Coder models also drop into Claude Code and Cline via an OpenAI-compatible endpoint.

Serving

vLLM and SGLang are the first-party production targets, with official recipes per model. llama.cpp, Ollama, LM Studio and MLX cover local use. Part 11 is a deep dive on the vLLM path.

Fine-tuning

Qwen bases are first-class in Unsloth, LLaMA-Factory, Axolotl, TRL and ms-swift. New generations typically get Unsloth and LLaMA-Factory support within days, and quantised GGUF/AWQ conversions within hours.

Beyond the LLM

The family extends well past text generation, and these siblings share the tokenizer and chat conventions: Qwen3-Embedding and Qwen3-Reranker (0.6B–8B, topped the MTEB multilingual leaderboard on release), Qwen3-VL-Embedding for multimodal retrieval, Qwen3Guard for streaming safety classification, Qwen-Image for generation and editing with unusually strong text rendering, Qwen3-ASR and Qwen-Audio-3.0 for speech, and Qwen-MT for translation across 92 languages.
